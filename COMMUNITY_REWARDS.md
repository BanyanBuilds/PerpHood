# Community and creator rewards — deferred in V20

The older 1% fee / 90% holder-reward proposal is not part of the V20 BattlePool engine.

V20 prioritizes one conserved TOKEN/WETH balance sheet, instant executable exits, reserve-capped leverage, and real liquidation price impact. The current 0.30% execution fee and actual residual liquidation equity remain inside BattlePool equity during simulation.

Current rules:

- no creator fee privilege,
- no free creator allocation,
- no creator reward merely for deploying,
- no active holder distribution,
- no reward extraction that weakens instant settlement.

A future holder, referral, or treasury route may be considered only after its worst-case effect on pool solvency is simulated, specified, and independently audited.
