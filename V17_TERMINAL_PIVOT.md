# PERPHOOD V17 — Persistent Terminal Pivot

- `/` is now the terminal and home experience.
- Launch, positions, alerts, watchlists, wallets, and settings open as terminal drawers.
- The former `/terminal` route redirects home.
- Seeded/demo market registry was removed; empty states remain honest until the live indexer returns data.
- Trading balance defaults to zero.
- Launch fields no longer contain demo content.
- GIF, animated WebP, and animated AVIF creator media are accepted and rendered as the token image itself.
- Animated token artwork remains animated in previews, market rows, headers, and avatars.
- Launch completion stays inside the terminal rather than navigating to a separate page.
