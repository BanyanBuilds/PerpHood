# Leverage X V39 — Clean Shell + Full-Row Market Navigation

V39 corrects the landing-terminal hierarchy after the V38 spacing pass.

## Command bar

- Replaced the crowded single flex row with three deliberate zones:
  - Leverage X brand + Markets/Movers
  - compact ticker/contract search + Paste CA
  - 360 Hz status + settings + funding + account
- Removed passive chain, position-count, and portfolio-value readouts from the top bar.
- Kept passive connection and engine state in the bottom utility bar and position/watchlist strip.
- Replaced the large “Trading account / Open sidebar” control with a compact account balance button.
- Increased shell height and horizontal spacing while preserving the no-page-scroll terminal.

## Market columns

- Added more space above the three-category deck.
- Increased outer gutters and inter-column gaps.
- Increased category-header height so controls and titles do not crowd one another.

## Token rows

- Clicking anywhere in a market row opens its token workspace unless the click originated from an actual button, link, input, or utility action.
- Enter and Space open the focused market row.
- Market cap/change, utility buttons, metrics, and Buy/Long/Short actions now occupy dedicated grid regions.
- Signals and metrics wrap instead of colliding or painting over the action buttons.
